3. Protocol for Resignation & Draw Offers

Matches on ChessHub follow precise client-server communication protocols to prevent griefing and unauthorized game cancellations:

Mutual Draw Agreement Protocol

A player may propose a draw through the in-game action menu. When triggered, the system presents a Waiting for opponent dialog to the initiator and delivers an interactive prompt to the receiving player (Opponent has offered a draw. Accept offer?). A draw is executed if and only if the opponent accepts. If declined or ignored, the match continues uninterrupted without rating penalty.

Voluntary Resignation Protocol

Players may voluntarily resign at any point during their turn or the opponent's turn. Resignation is final, irrevocable, and immediately terminates the match. The game room triggers an immediate game-over state, awarding the victory to the opponent and committing the updated ELO scores to Firestore. Resignations cannot be recalled or disputed.

When utilizing the Over-The-Board Dual Clock feature on a single shared physical device, both players share mutual responsibility for physical clock tap toggling, turn progression, and fair play.
